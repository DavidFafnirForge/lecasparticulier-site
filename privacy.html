Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This application ("the App") is a private publishing tool used by the owner of the TikTok account @LeCasParticulier to send videos to that same account. It has no other users.

1. Data the App accesses

Through TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API, and only after the account owner authorises it, the App accesses:

  • basic profile information (open ID, display name, username) — to confirm which account is being posted to;
  • the permission to upload video files to that account's inbox (scope video.upload).

The App does not read messages, followers, or analytics, and does not access any other TikTok account.

2. Data the App stores

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, stored on the owner's private server, in a file readable only by the service account;
  • the video files to be uploaded, on the same server, until they are sent and archived.

No personal data of any third party is collected. No data is sold, rented or shared.

3. Data retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner revokes access, which can be done at any time from the TikTok account settings (Security & permissions → Manage app permissions) or by deleting the token file on the server. Video files remain on the owner's server and are deleted at the owner's discretion. To request deletion, use the contact address below.

4. Third parties

The App transmits video files to TikTok, whose own privacy policy applies to what TikTok then stores and displays. The App uses no analytics service and no advertising service.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone other than the account owner.
